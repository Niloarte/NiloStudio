Cookie Policy
Last updated: January 2025  ·  niloarte.com

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to store data that can be recalled by a web server. We use cookies to remember your preferences, analyse site traffic, and ensure the website functions securely.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Necessary Cookies: Required for fundamental site functionality, navigation, and security. The site cannot function correctly without them.
  • Analytical Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with the website by collecting and reporting information anonymously (e.g. visit counts, page duration).

3. Third-Party Cookies

We use embedded services from third parties, such as Vimeo for video playback. These providers may place cookies on your device when you play a video to track usage statistics and user preferences. We do not control these third-party cookies. Please consult Vimeo's cookie policy for details.

4. Managing Cookie Consent

When you first visit the Website, you will see a consent banner. You can choose to "Accept All" or "Reject" non-essential cookies. You can update your preferences or withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser cache or clicking the policy links in the footer.
